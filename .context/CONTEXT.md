# Context: Aivanta Website & Public Documentation

## 1. Executive Summary
Aivanta is an independent open technology initiative dedicated to advancing trustworthy AI, digital autonomy, and open technology through independent research and engineering. This repository delivers the public website and organizational documentation published at [https://aivanta-project.github.io](https://aivanta-project.github.io).

## 2. Problem Statement & Motivation
Modern AI systems operate with opaque execution histories, centralized runtime control, and insufficient user auditability. Aivanta conducts public-interest research and builds open-source software that delivers verifiable cryptographic execution traces (SignalTrace) and preserves user sovereignty across computing environments.

## 3. Position in the Ecosystem
- **Public Gateway:** Communicates Aivanta's mission, values, research themes, and governance model.
- **Project Directory:** Showcases active initiatives, specifications, and repositories.
- **Reference Evidence:** Serves as official public documentation for applications, grants, and academic research collaborations.

## 4. Key Stakeholders & Users
- Open-source engineers and cryptographers.
- AI safety researchers and systems architects.
- Prospective contributors and organizational partners.
