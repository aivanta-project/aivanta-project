01 / About Aivanta

Independent by design.
Open by principle.

Aivanta is an independent open technology initiative exploring how research and practical engineering can make advanced technology more accessible, trustworthy and accountable.

We create and support open-source software, investigate emerging technical challenges, and develop reusable public-interest technology. Our projects are independently useful and open to inspection, experimentation and contribution.
